AFK Redeemer Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026

Developer: AtelierCode

1. Introduction

AFK Redeemer is a fan-made app built by players for players. This privacy policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and what rights you have as a user.

2. Data We Collect

Data you provide directly:

  • Your AFK Arena UID (Classic accounts) — stored locally on your device only, never transmitted to our servers.
  • Your Lilith Games email address (email login) — used solely to authenticate with Lilith Games' redemption portal. It is stored locally on your device so you can manage multiple logins and stay signed in, and it is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Lilith Games session tokens — issued by Lilith Games after you log in, stored in your device's secure storage and sent only to Lilith Games, never to our servers.

Data collected automatically:

  • Firebase Analytics: anonymous usage data (app opens, feature usage, redemption outcomes such as the number of codes redeemed, ad performance, and whether an in-app review prompt was shown). No personally identifiable information is collected.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: anonymous crash and error reports to help us improve stability.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: a device token is stored on our servers together with the notification topics you follow, your platform (Android/iOS) and the app version, solely to send push notifications about new redemption codes and app updates. This token is not linked to any personal identity.

Redemption feedback:

  • When you redeem a code, the result of the redemption (success, expired, not found) is sent anonymously to our database to help verify codes for the entire community. No account information is included in this data.
  • If you enter a code manually, the code itself may be sent anonymously to our database so it can be verified and shared with the community. No account information is included.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any personal identity on our servers.
  • We do not collect your Lilith Games password. Authentication with Lilith Games' servers happens directly between your device and Lilith Games — AFK Redeemer never sees or stores your password.
  • We do not sell, share, or trade any user data with third parties.
  • We do not collect payment information. In-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

4. Third-Party Services

AFK Redeemer uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — displays advertisements to free-tier users. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization. (https://policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Ad partners in AdMob mediation — the app includes the SDKs of the following ad networks, which AdMob may call to serve ads in some countries. When their SDK is active, they may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and interactions with ads, as described in their own policies:
    • Yango Ads (Yandex Mobile Ads SDK, which includes AppMetrica) — serves ads to users in Russia. (https://yango.com/legal/yango_ads_privacy/)
    • Liftoff Monetize (Vungle). (https://liftoff.io/privacy-policy/)
    Where consent is required (for example in the EEA and the UK), ads are personalized only if you allow it in the consent form, which you can reopen from Settings.
  • Firebase (Google) — analytics, crash reporting, push notifications, and database. (https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
  • Google Play — in-app purchase processing. (https://policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Lilith Games — redemption requests are sent directly to Lilith Games' servers. (https://www.lilithgames.com/privacy-policy)

5. Data Retention

  • Your UID, your Lilith Games email address and your session tokens are stored locally on your device and can be removed at any time by removing the login, logging out or uninstalling the app.
  • FCM device tokens are retained on our servers until the token becomes invalid or you uninstall the app.
  • Anonymous analytics and crash data are retained according to Firebase's standard retention policies.

6. Children's Privacy

AFK Redeemer is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. AFK Arena itself is rated for users 12 and older. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

You may request deletion of any data associated with your device by contacting us at the email address below. Since most data is anonymous, deletion requests are typically fulfilled by clearing your device token from our FCM database.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes via a push notification or an in-app notice. The effective date at the top of this page will always reflect the latest version.
