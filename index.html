AFK Redeemer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Developer: Ateliersoft

1. Data Controller

The AFK Redeemer app is published by Ateliersoft, the brand under which an independent developer publishes their apps. For any request concerning this policy, please use the developer contact details shown on the app's Google Play listing.

AFK Redeemer is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with Lilith Games.

2. Data Processed and Purposes

The app does not require an account with us. The game accounts, preferences and other content entered in the app are stored on the user's device.

When using the app, the following categories of data may be processed:

  • Game identifiers and login data entered by the user (for example the game UID or the email address used with the game publisher's account, and the related session data), to redeem gift codes: they are transmitted to the game publisher's service for that purpose, and that processing is governed by the publisher's own privacy policy.
  • Data on gift codes and redemption outcomes, including codes entered manually, not linked to the user's identity or game accounts, to keep the list of codes up to date.
  • Technical and usage data (for example device type, system and app version, interactions with the app, error reports), to provide the service, ensure its proper operation and improve it.
  • Device identifiers, to deliver notifications, where the user allows them, and to show ads in the free version.
  • Data provided voluntarily when the user contacts us (for example the message and the email address), to handle the request.

3. Third-Party Services

The app relies on services provided by Google (including advertising, usage statistics, error reporting, notifications, data storage, purchases and updates through Google Play) and on other advertising networks that may serve ads through Google AdMob, including Yango Ads and Liftoff Monetize. These parties process data under their own policies:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Yango Ads: https://yango.com/legal/yango_ads_privacy/
  • Liftoff Monetize: https://liftoff.io/privacy-policy/

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, with the safeguards required by applicable law.

4. Advertising and Consent

Where required by applicable law, the app asks for the user's consent before showing personalised ads; consent can be changed at any time from the app. The advertising ID can be reset or limited in the device settings.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not receive the user's payment details.

6. Legal Basis and Retention

Data is processed on the basis of the user's consent, where required, the need to provide the service requested by the user, and the legitimate interest in its operation and improvement. It is kept for as long as necessary for the stated purposes.

7. Your Rights

Users may request access to, correction or deletion of their data, object to or restrict its processing, and lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority. Requests can be sent through the contact details above and will be answered within the time limits set by law.

8. Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page with the date of the update.

9. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please use the developer contact details shown on the app's Google Play listing.
